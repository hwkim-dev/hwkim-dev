## PROFILE

I am Hyunwoo Kim, President of [Altifigence](https://altifigence.com), building the PCCX AI project family around memory-bound Transformer inference, FPGA/NPU architecture, and practical edge AI systems.

This profile is the public engineering surface for company direction, product architecture, technical notes, and open implementation work. I keep the emphasis on material that can be reviewed through code, documentation, and reproducible experiments.

| AREA | CURRENT FOCUS |
|---|---|
| [Altifigence](https://altifigence.com) | Company direction, product architecture, and technical publication. |
| [PCCX AI](https://github.com/pccxai) | NPU architecture, tooling, and documentation for memory-bound Transformer inference. |
| [hkimw](https://github.com/hkimw) | Engineering notes, experiments, and portfolio source. |
